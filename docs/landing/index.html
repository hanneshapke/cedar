What it does

Real tasks, done for you.
Not just answers.

Cedar searches the web, reads your files, runs the numbers and writes the result: a report, a spreadsheet, a map, a chart. For bigger tasks you see the plan before the work starts, and the sources when it's done.

01

Works on its own, even while you sleep

Turn any conversation into an agent that runs every morning at 7, or when something happens: a price drops, an email arrives, a meeting is about to start. It tells your desktop or phone when it's done.

1Record your screen while you do it once
2Read the steps Cedar wrote, correct them, save them

02

Learns your actions, and uses the computer privately

When there's no tool for a job, show Cedar how: record your screen while you do it once, and it writes down the steps and follows them next time. For what only exists behind windows, it works a desktop with the mouse and keyboard: one in a container, or your own, where it asks before it acts.

03

Private chats, gone when you leave

For something you want neither remembered nor kept. A private chat is in no list, search or inbox. Cedar brings nothing from memory into it and saves nothing from it, and it is deleted with its files when you leave it. On the web and in the phone apps.

04

Trusted execution, in containers and OpenShell

Whatever the model runs, it runs in a container of the conversation's own, with no network unless you allow one. On Linux and macOS you can turn on NVIDIA OpenShell, which checks every connection: which program wants to reach which host. What it refuses, Cedar reports and asks you about.

05

Every ability has a switch

Weather, maps, recipes, directions and transit, stocks, shopping, your email and calendar. Turn on what you want Cedar to reach. Everything else stays off.

Private by design

Your assistant.
Your machine. Your data.

Most assistants run in someone else's cloud. Cedar runs at home, and treats every task like a stranger in the house.

Stays on your machine

The model, your conversations and your files live on your machine. No account needed, nothing to subscribe to.

Each chat in a sealed box

Every conversation gets its own workspace that can't see your documents, your photos or the internet unless you allow it. Between messages it sleeps; delete the chat and the box is gone.

Asks before anything risky

Sending an email, downloading a file, deleting something: Cedar stops and asks. You approve once, or always, or never.

Yours to shape

Pick the brain.
Add the hands.

Cedar isn't tied to one AI company. Run a model on your Mac or PC with Ollama or LM Studio, serve one from a Linux GPU box with vLLM, or point it at a server you trust. Switch between them per conversation.

No model server yet? On your Mac or PC with a capable GPU, Cedar downloads a model and runs it for you with llama.cpp.

Need something specific? Add a tool from the community with one click, after seeing exactly what it asks for. The first one reads an insulin pump and compares your day with your week.

The iPhone app, privately

No invite, and no email address to share: install Cedar yourself with SideStore, a free, open source app store that signs it on your iPhone with your own Apple ID. A free Apple ID is enough.

  1. Install SideStore once, following the guide on sidestore.io. Only this first install needs a computer.
  2. On the iPhone, add Cedar's source: tap Add to SideStore, or in SideStore go to Sources → + and paste {{ sourceUrl }}
  3. Tap Cedar, then Free to install it. SideStore offers each new release as an update.
  4. Pair it with your computer as above: scan the QR code from Settings → Scan QR Code.

Rather install the file yourself? Download the .ipa on the iPhone and open it with SideStore (My Apps → +). The app is the same as on TestFlight except for push notifications, which an app signed with your own Apple ID can't have. With a free Apple ID, SideStore refreshes it every 7 days.

Compared

Cedar, OpenClaw, OpenBot, Meta Muse,
OpenAI Dots, Claude Duet and Claude Managed Agents.

Seven ways to have an agent do tasks for you. OpenClaw lives in your chat apps and acts on your computer. OpenBot runs a team of agents such as Codex and Claude Code on your computer. Meta Muse runs in Meta’s cloud. OpenAI Dots is an always-on agent on its own computer in OpenAI’s cloud. Claude Duet is Anthropic’s, a proactive agent on its own computer in Anthropic’s cloud. Self-hosted Claude Managed Agents is Anthropic’s agent platform for companies, run on their own servers while the model stays with Anthropic or a cloud provider. Cedar runs at home, on a Mac, a Windows PC or a Linux box, and keeps every task in its own sealed box.

Setup

Install Cedar. It sets up the rest.

Download Cedar and open it. Its setup window installs Docker for the sealed box Cedar works in, and gets you a model. Nothing to prepare first. On Linux it is a few commands, and the install guide has them for your computer.

Cedar already runs on another computer of yours? Connect this one to it: it needs no Docker and no model of its own.

Your model, three ways

Cedar runs a model for you

Easiest

With a capable graphics card or an Apple silicon Mac, Cedar downloads llama.cpp and a model that fits, and runs them. Nothing to install first, and nothing leaves your computer.

A hosted provider

No big computer? Choose Baseten, Nebius, Doubleword or another provider in the setup window and paste an API key. They run the model, and you pay for what you use.

You run the model server

Already run Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp or vLLM? Click its preset in the setup window. The install guide suggests a model for your hardware.
