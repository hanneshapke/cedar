Halvorsen Bridge

The Halvorsen Bridge carries Harbor Road across the Alder Narrows. It is a two-hinged steel arch with a main span of 312 metres, the longest in the region when it was built.

History

Construction began in 1927 after a ferry accident on the Narrows. The bridge was designed by the structural engineer Ingrid Solberg, one of the first women licensed as a civil engineer in the province. Work was delayed for two years by the collapse of a temporary falsework tower, and the bridge finally opened to traffic on 14 May 1931.

It was named after Anders Halvorsen, the ferry captain who died in the 1925 accident.

Visiting

A pedestrian walkway on the east side is open from 6am to 10pm.
