Contribute a conversation

Give one of your ChatGPT or Claude conversations to Cedar's open corpus, which trains an open-weight agent model. Your export is read in this page and is never uploaded. Only the one conversation you review and send leaves your browser.

  • One at a time. You read every conversation before it goes. There is no "send all".
  • Redacted here. Keys, emails, phone and card numbers, IP addresses and user names are replaced with fake values of the same shape. You can add your own phrases.
  • No account. You can stay anonymous or use a random pseudonym, and you can withdraw a contribution later.

1 Load your export

How to export from ChatGPT
  1. In ChatGPT, open Settings → Data controls.
  2. Choose Export data and confirm. OpenAI emails you a download link, usually within minutes.
  3. Download the .zip and drop it above. You don't need to unpack it.
How to export from Claude
  1. On claude.ai, open Settings → Privacy.
  2. Choose Export data. Anthropic emails you a download link.
  3. Download the .zip and drop it above. Claude's export doesn't say which model answered, so you'll be asked.
